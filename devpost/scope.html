Untitled roadmap tool Scope approved

A roadmap tool for Indian undergrads chasing fully funded internships abroad. It shows which real programs you can actually apply to, how ready you really are for each, and a plan that fits around your university timetable.

Finish line, in your words

“see that roadmap and plan according to my internships and my university timetable”scope.md › What “Working” Looks Like

The unique kernel: three real inputs, one plan

Real program requirementsQuoted from official pages: skills, documents (SOP, recommendation letters), and in some cases a professor's acceptance.
Your verified levelFrom a short interview that checks what you can actually do. Not a self-rating.
Your timetableExam weeks and free hours, so each step lands in a week you can actually do it.

Study planners don't know the programs. Program lists don't know you. Neither knows your semester.

Core loop

Who it's for

The person

A 3rd-year B.Tech student at an Indian university (starting with VIT Pune), applying this cycle to MITACS Globalink, DAAD WISE, Charpak, or HENNGE. Has finished Python; doesn't know what to learn next or what research involves.

What they do today

Piece it together from scattered official pages, seniors' advice, and YouTube, then guess what to do in which week around exams.

Proof-of-concept boundary

NowBuilt in the proof of concept
  • A small curated set of fully funded programs open to Indian students. Every requirement has its official source and a quoted line.
  • A taste test using real project descriptions from programs that publish them.
  • An eligibility check: nationality, year, CGPA, branch.
  • A short AI readiness interview against what the programs need.
  • Timetable entered by hand: exam weeks and free hours per week.
  • A roadmap per program, scheduled around timetable and deadlines, with one highlighted next step.
LaterWorth doing, not now
  • Reading a timetable from a photo (stretch goal, decided at the Oct 17 checkpoint).
  • A “re-check this program” button that reads the official page live and shows “verified today”.
  • More programs and countries; other universities' calendars.
CutExplicitly out, with reasons
  • Visa-approval odds: no reliable public data, and a made-up probability wouldn't hold up. Official visa notes are shown instead.
  • SOP drafting: not the problem being solved, and it raises honesty issues.
  • Professor finder / cold emails: a separate, large problem.
  • Reminders and notifications: not needed to prove the core idea.
  • Accounts and login: not needed for a proof of concept.
If behindCut order at the Oct 17 checkpoint
  • The readiness interview becomes a simple self-check first. The taste test, eligibility check, and timetable-fitted roadmap stay.

Identity & disclosure

Feel

Its own product, not a Scout reskin and not generic “AI app” styling. Honest and trustworthy: every requirement shows where it came from. Visual direction gets decided in the PRD.
