Untitled roadmap tool PRD approved

Find a direction, see which real funded programs you can apply to, check how ready you are, and get a plan built backwards from each program's official selection stages, fitted around your exams.

This page also previews the agreed look: paper planner base with bold accents.

What got sharper since scope

Scope: "a roadmap per program, fitted to your timetable."
→
Now: roadmaps grouped into Research and Company tracks; shared steps appear once and serve several programs; each track is built from real selection stages (professor acceptance, documents, coding test, interview); expired and at-risk deadlines are handled explicitly.

The core journey

First: a reusable taste test grounded in real work genres. Next: Scout-like preference questions. Then: discover, filter and choose internships, followed by the already-agreed readiness, timetable and grouped roadmaps.

Deadlines never expire unnoticed

Product decisions (yours)

Group by trackResearch and Company roadmaps, so the same prep isn't planned three times.
Built from selection stagesYour point: the goal is getting selected, so official stages are the milestones.
Earliest-deadline schedulingShared steps land before the earliest deadline they serve.
Expired / at-risk handled"it should not happen like deadline of one or more internship in that group expire"
Taste test → preferences → choicesReal genre questions first, Scout-like search questions next. Discover and filter internships; the student selects targets. No fixed program list.
Official sources onlyReview sites left out: sparse, unverifiable, scraping-restricted.
Interview = level checkMock interview comes later.
Paper planner + bold accentsGen Z study-planner look, ready-made components; you'll fine-tune the UI by hand.

Boundary

NowWhat we're building
  • Taste test (~10–12 reusable, source-grounded genre questions) with a direction result
  • Scout-like preference questions, discovery, filters and student-selected targets, rebuilt fresh without Scout code or integration
  • TinyFish powers fresh Scout-style discovery: explainable ranking, result filters, save/hide controls and search/source progress. Bookmarking is separate from roadmap target selection.
  • Eligibility checks against discovered internships' official criteria, with quoted sources and visible unknowns
  • Manual timetable entry (exam weeks, free hours)
  • Readiness interview with tested-skill statuses; uncovered skills are Not assessed, documents/external actions have checklists
  • Roadmap overview with one feasible highlighted step, or an honest empty/exam/waiting state
  • Track timeline from official selection stages, with shared steps, deadlines, exam weeks and done/not done
  • Closed and at-risk deadline handling; source tag on every requirement
DeferredNot in the proof of concept
  • Accounts / sync across devices
  • Per-program filter on the timeline
  • Mock interview
  • Student-reported interview experiences
  • Timetable from a photo (Oct 17 checkpoint)
Non-goalsWill not do
  • Visa-approval odds
  • SOP drafting
  • Professor finder / cold emails (shown as a stage, not solved)
  • Reminders
  • Scraping review sites
Final reviewClarified states and limits
  • Coming back: same-browser progress; resume the in-progress screen, or the overview when a roadmap exists.
  • No eligible programs: sourced reasons, visible unconfirmed conditions and a route to revise preferences.
  • All eligible programs closed: closed-cycle history and general preparation; next openings only with official evidence.
  • Rolling programs: missed personal targets are overdue, not proof that the program closed.
  • Funding: fully funded focus with editable filters; show fees, caps and exclusions, not a zero-cost promise.
  • Nothing marked interested in the taste test: say so and continue with all fields open.
  • Project name: still yours to pick; can wait until before submission.
